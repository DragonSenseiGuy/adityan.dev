Most of what I make starts as a small itch, like a bot that would make a community more fun or a tool that saves me ten minutes a day, and turns into something shipped. Much of it happens through Hack Club, a worldwide community of teenage makers, where I've built a community Slack bot (Dragon Bot) and shipped through YSWS programs.

Lately I've been focused on AI-assisted developer tools, like Hack Review, a GitHub App that reviews pull requests automatically, and Kira, a Vue chat app for language models.

Away from AI, I tinker with self-hosting and infrastructure. I run a homelab off an old laptop and built Smart RSS, a minimalist feed aggregator, to go with it.
