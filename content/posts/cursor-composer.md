About Cursor Composer
---------------------

Cursor Composer is an advanced AI-powered coding assistant that helps developers write, edit, and understand code more efficiently. It represents a significant evolution in AI-assisted development tools.

Key Features
------------

*   **Multi-file editing:** Composer can make coordinated changes across multiple files in your codebase, understanding the relationships between different parts of your project.
*   **Speed:** It is one of the fastest and most accurate models ever.

Benefits
--------

Cursor Composer speeds up development by reducing context switching and automating repetitive tasks. It lets developers focus on high-level architecture and problem-solving while the AI handles implementation details. The tool excels at rapidly prototyping ideas, maintaining consistency across large codebases, and helping teams onboard to new projects.
